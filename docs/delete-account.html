Hapus Akun & Data Zettask

Pengembang: Zetheron Aplikasi: com.zetheron.zettask Kontak: zetheron.id@gmail.com

Anda dapat menghapus akun Zettask beserta seluruh data cloud secara permanen kapan saja, baik langsung dari dalam aplikasi maupun melalui email jika aplikasi sudah dicopot.

Opsi 1: Hapus Langsung dari Aplikasi (Instan)

  1. Buka aplikasi Zettask di perangkat Android Anda (pastikan Anda dalam keadaan masuk ke akun).
  2. Ketuk tab More (Workspace) di bilah navigasi bawah.
  3. Ketuk Kartu Akun di bagian paling atas.
  4. Ketuk tombol Hapus Akun.
  5. Selesaikan Konfirmasi Ganda yang muncul di layar. Jika diminta demi keamanan, keluar lalu masuk kembali terlebih dahulu sebelum mengonfirmasi.

Opsi 2: Permintaan Hapus Akun via Email (Jika Aplikasi Sudah Dicopot)

Jika Anda tidak lagi memasang Zettask di perangkat Anda, kirim permintaan penghapusan akun dari alamat email yang terdaftar pada akun Zettask Anda:

  • Kirim ke: zetheron.id@gmail.com
  • Subjek: Permintaan Hapus Akun Zettask
  • Isi pesan: Cantumkan alamat email akun Zettask Anda dan nyatakan bahwa Anda meminta penghapusan akun serta seluruh data cloud secara permanen.
Kirim Email Permintaan Hapus Akun

Data yang Dihapus Secara Permanen

Saat penghapusan akun diproses, seluruh data berikut dihapus secara permanen dari server kami:

  • Akun Firebase Authentication: UID, alamat email, nama tampilan, dan tautan metode login (Email/Password atau Google).
  • Seluruh dokumen & sub-koleksi Cloud Firestore pada users/{uid}:
    • tasks (seluruh tugas dan sub-tugas)
    • repeating_tasks (aturan tugas berulang)
    • projects (proyek di Board)
    • tags (tag warna)
    • notes (catatan cepat)
    • task_templates (template tugas)

Estimasi Waktu Proses & Data Lokal

Metode Estimasi Waktu Retensi Data Cloud
Hapus di dalam aplikasi Instan (segera setelah konfirmasi ganda) 0 hari (tidak ada cadangan tersisa)
Permintaan via email Maksimal 7 hari kerja setelah verifikasi email 0 hari setelah proses selesai
Catatan Penting: Penghapusan akun dan data cloud tidak dapat dibatalkan. Data lokal di perangkat Anda (database SQLite) tetap aman hingga Anda menghapus data aplikasi di Pengaturan Android atau mencopot aplikasi Zettask.

Delete Your Zettask Account & Data

Developer: Zetheron App: com.zetheron.zettask Contact: zetheron.id@gmail.com

You can permanently delete your Zettask account and all synced cloud data at any time, either directly inside the app or by email if you have already uninstalled the app.

Option 1: Delete Directly in the App (Instant)

  1. Open Zettask on your Android device (while signed in to your account).
  2. Tap the More (Workspace) tab in the bottom navigation bar.
  3. Tap your Account Card at the top of the screen.
  4. Tap Delete Account.
  5. Complete the Double Confirmation prompts. For security, if asked to re-authenticate, sign out and sign back in, then repeat step 4.

Option 2: Request Deletion via Email (If App Is Uninstalled)

If you no longer have Zettask installed, send a deletion request from the email address registered to your Zettask account:

  • Send to: zetheron.id@gmail.com
  • Subject: Zettask Account Deletion Request
  • Body: Include your registered email address and confirm that you request permanent deletion of your account and cloud data.
Email Deletion Request

Data Permanently Deleted

When your account deletion is processed, all of the following records are permanently removed from our servers:

  • Firebase Authentication account: UID, email address, display name, and linked sign-in provider (Email/Password or Google).
  • All Cloud Firestore documents & sub-collections under users/{uid}:
    • tasks (all tasks and checklists)
    • repeating_tasks (repeating routines)
    • projects (Board projects)
    • tags (color tags)
    • notes (quick notes)
    • task_templates (task templates)

Processing Time & Local Device Data

Method Estimated Processing Time Cloud Data Retention
In-app deletion Instant (immediately after double confirmation) 0 days (no cloud backups retained)
Email request Up to 7 business days after email verification 0 days upon completion
Important: Account and cloud data deletion cannot be undone. Local data stored on your device (SQLite database) remains intact until you clear app storage in Android Settings or uninstall Zettask.
